Method and applicability

Lagrange interpolation

L(x)=∑iyi∏j≠ix−xjxi−xj

Distinct x nodes define a unique polynomial of degree at most n − 1. Extrapolation and high-degree interpolation may be unreliable.

Results

Solution report

Worked example

(0, 0), (1, 1), (2, 4) → L(x) = x² → L(1.5) = 2.25

Calculation steps

  1. ℓ₀(x) = (x − 1)(x − 2)/2; ℓ₁(x) = −x(x − 2); ℓ₂(x) = x(x − 1)/2
  2. L(x) = 0·ℓ₀(x) + ℓ₁(x) + 4ℓ₂(x)
  3. L(x) = −x(x − 2) + 2x(x − 1) = x²
  4. L(1.5) = 2.25

Accuracy and common pitfalls

Barycentric evaluation is preferable to expanded coefficients, especially for large or close nodes. It does not eliminate high-degree oscillations or unreliable extrapolation.
